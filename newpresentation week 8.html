01 — FIRST EXPERIENCES

My First Experiences in Korea

🇰🇷 My First Days in Korea

A Completely New Experience

When I first came to Korea, everything felt new to me. The language, people, transportation, buildings, food, and even the daily lifestyle were different from what I was familiar with.

During my first days, I was excited but also a little nervous. I had to learn how things worked and become comfortable in a completely new environment.

These first experiences helped me become more independent and gave me the opportunity to learn something new every day.

"Every new place teaches you something about the world and something about yourself."

03 — ADAPTING TO LIFE

Adjusting My Life to a New Environment

Learning to Adapt

Living in a new country means learning how to adjust to a different environment. At first, some things were difficult because they were different from my previous lifestyle.

Slowly, I started understanding the transportation system, daily routines, weather, social environment, and the way people communicate.

I learned that adapting does not happen immediately. It takes time, patience, and a willingness to learn.
